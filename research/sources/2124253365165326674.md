### Ramp on how to get AI to recommend you

**Build a second version of your site just for AI**
- Ramp put an offer on about 50 pages that only AI bots could see.
- Human visitors saw nothing different.
- Then they tracked whether ChatGPT, Claude and Perplexity would pass the offer on to real buyers.

**Markdown beats schema**
- They tested 3 formats: Markdown, plain stripped-down HTML and pages packed with schema.
- Everyone expected schema, the code made for machines, to win.
- Markdown was the only format that reliably showed up in AI answers.

**Find out who's really visiting**
- Thousands of visits from 7 different AI platforms in just 2 weeks.
- Some AI bots pretend to be normal browsers. DeepSeek shows up as a Chrome version from 2017.
- Claude's crawler visited more than all the other named AI crawlers combined.

**Ask buyer questions every single day**
- Ask the AI tools the same questions a CFO would ask when picking a spend management tool.
- Then check every answer for your offer.

**Every AI behaves differently**
- Claude passed on the exact offer, the link and step-by-step instructions.
- Perplexity only mentioned it vaguely.
- ChatGPT read the pages but mentioned the offer 0 times in 32 days.
- One strategy for "AI" doesn't work. Each model needs its own plan.

**AI trusts pages it already cites**
- Pages AI was already citing were the ones where the offer showed up.
- Pages with few AI citations got zero mentions, whatever the format.
- One of the best pages was one Ramp never cared about for Google.

**The results**
- 1,300+ AI bot visits in the first 2.5 weeks.
- About 40 AI answers mentioned the offer at 2.5 weeks.
- 370 by day 32, almost 10x.
- A peak of 33 mentions in a single day.

**That's the whole game now**
- Ramp didn't win this by writing more blogs.
- They figured out which pages AI already trusts, then put the right message there.
- If AI doesn't already trust the pages talking about you, it won't recommend you.

**Get mentioned where AI already looks**
- Writing more blogs isn't the move. Getting cited on the right pages is.
- Find out which pages ChatGPT and Claude already trust in your niche.
- Get your brand mentioned on those pages.
- Pages with few AI citations got zero mentions, so skip them and go where AI already cites.
