Points the course cites, paraphrased:

- Write line 1 for a stranger. Three openers: the problem the tool removes, the test you ran, or what someone can now do in one sentence. Name the product on line 2.
- Swap adjectives for numbers people can check. Sume's launch compared markups on one video model in a vertical list, with the competitors' calculators shown in the video. Put the benchmark code in the first reply.
- Leo's storage benchmark: 137.8K views; part 2 in the same format: 18.5K. Launch something small every week.
- A 45-second demo is about 105 words. Open on the payoff, show the old way, then the fast demo, and end on the command or URL. The clock credits a breakdown by Farza and Aaron Epstein.
